From First Scan To Final Smile — Here's The Whole Process

SmileArc pairs an easy, at-home experience with real oversight from US-licensed orthodontists at every stage. Here's exactly what to expect.

Before You Start

What Can SmileArc Aligners Fix?

Your call with our team will confirm your specific case, but these are the alignment issues SmileArc treats most often.

Underbite

Your lower front teeth sit further forward than your upper front teeth when you bite down.

Gap Teeth

Noticeable spacing between two or more teeth, most often between the upper front teeth.

Open Bite

Your upper and lower front teeth don't touch at all when your back teeth are closed together.

Crowded Teeth

Teeth that overlap, twist, or bunch up because there isn't quite enough room along the jaw.

Crossbite

One or more upper teeth sit inside your lower teeth instead of outside them when you bite down.

Overbite

Your upper front teeth extend noticeably further forward than your lower front teeth.

As soon as your deposit is confirmed, we ship your precision at-home impression kit — everything you need to capture your bite from your kitchen table, no dental office required.

  • Photo shows exactly what arrives at your door
Step 5

Once your kit is in hand, we'll book a live video call on Google Meet or Zoom to walk you through capturing your impressions step-by-step, in real time.

  • Live video call, about 15 minutes

Step 6

Your Plan Is Built By A Licensed Orthodontist

A US-licensed orthodontist reviews your impressions and designs a custom treatment plan — mapping out every tooth movement from your first aligner to your last. You'll get a 3D preview of your projected results before your aligners are made.

  • See your projected new smile in 3D
  • Confirm your final treatment details
  • Adjust the plan before manufacturing begins
Step 7

Wear, Track, And Watch Your Smile Change

Once your plan is approved, we collect your remaining balance and your full set of custom aligners ships to your door. Wear each tray 20–22 hours a day, switch to the next set every 1–2 weeks, and check in with your care team through the SmileArc app as often as you need.

  • Progress photo check-ins built into the app
  • Unlimited messaging with your care team
  • Custom retainers to protect your results

Common Questions

Quick Answers About The Process

Does the at-home kit hurt or feel uncomfortable?+

No — the impression kit uses a soft, single-use tray with a fast-setting material. Most members finish both impressions in under 10 minutes with the included video guide.

What if my case is too complex for aligners?+

Your intake and impressions are reviewed by a licensed orthodontist before any plan is built. If SmileArc isn't the right fit, we'll tell you upfront and won't charge you anything.

How often do I talk to my care team?+

As often as you'd like. You get unlimited in-app messaging plus scheduled photo check-ins throughout treatment, and can request a live video call any time.

How does the 40% deposit work?+

A 40% deposit reserves your plan and starts your impression kit on its way. The remaining 60% isn't charged until your aligners are approved and ready to ship.

When does the video call happen?+

After your impression kit arrives at your door. We'll walk you through capturing your impressions live on Google Meet or Zoom — there's no video call before that.
