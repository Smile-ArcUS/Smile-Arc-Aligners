Why SmileArc

Clinical Care Meets Everyday Convenience

We built SmileArc around one idea: you shouldn't have to choose between a doctor-directed treatment plan and a process that actually fits your life.

Doctor-Directed Plans

Every SmileArc treatment plan is designed and monitored by a US-licensed orthodontist from your very first scan to your final retainer.

Made For Real Schedules

Skip the office visits entirely. Tell us your smile goal, we'll call you directly with your plan, and your impression kit ships to your door — with a live video call once it arrives.

Real-Results Guarantee

If your results don't match your treatment plan, we'll work with you on additional aligners at no extra cost — that's our SmileArc promise.

The Aligner Itself

One Product. Built To Actually Wear Every Day.

SmileArc makes one thing, and we've spent years getting it right: a clear aligner tray that's precision-fit to your teeth, comfortable enough to forget you're wearing, and clear enough that most people won't notice you are.

  • 3D-printed from BPA-free, medical-grade trilayer material
  • Smooth, rounded edges — no rough spots on cheeks or gums
  • Virtually invisible on video calls and in photos
  • Custom-fit to your scan for steady, gentle tooth movement
  • A fresh tray every 1–2 weeks — worn 20–22 hours a day
